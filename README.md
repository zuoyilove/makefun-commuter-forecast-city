Write one line of weather, 140 characters at most, for a city that does not exist. A judge playing a bored commuter picks the line that makes them smile. A Python program writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt`: broadcast weather for the invented city of Widdershins, where the 8:15 bus comes exactly when you stop checking your watch.

> Today in Widdershins: scattered applause, a high of 21°, and the 8:15 bus comes exactly when you stop checking your watch.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
